# Statio Core compared with Booksy, Fresha, Vagaro, Square Appointments, GlossGenius, Mangomint and Salonist

*A free, open-source alternative to Booksy and Fresha for salons, barbers, nail studios and spas.*

Most salon software is rented per person, per month, and some of it takes a share of new clients.
Statio Core is different: it is free software you run on your own accounts, so the website, the
booking page and the client list are yours. This page compares it honestly with the tools salons
use most, including where it falls short.

Prices below are the published US list prices we could find in **October 2026**, before tax and card
fees. They change often: please check each vendor's own pricing page before you decide.

## When Statio Core is the right choice

- You want **your own website and booking page on your own domain**, not a profile on a marketplace.
- You want to **own your client list** and never pay a share of new clients.
- You are comfortable with a little setup (Vercel, a database, an email key), or someone helps you
  with it. Claude Code can walk you through it ("Set up my salon").
- You want to see and change the code, or have a developer adapt it.

## When it isn't

Statio Core is not the best fit if you need **card payments and a till** in the same app: it records
the amount and how they paid, it doesn't take payments.

## Moving from Booksy, Fresha or Vagaro

Statio Core imports clients from a CSV: phone contacts, Excel, Booksy and Fresha exports
(*Clients › Import*). Services can be added from a price list with one command, or with Claude from a
photo of your price list. See the [How-to guide](GUIDE.md).
